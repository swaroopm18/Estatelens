# EstateLens — Seller Demo Review Flow

## Demo seller shortcut
From the landing page choose **Seller** and then choose **Demo Seller Account**.

The demo seller is preloaded for the academic review so the buyer-to-seller workflow can be shown without first creating or sourcing a separate seller account.

## Reviewer demonstration
1. Open the website and choose **Seller**.
2. Click **Demo Seller Account**.
3. Open **My Listings** to show the preloaded properties.
4. Open **Property Search** in a separate buyer browser/session.
5. Search for one of the demo property localities.
6. Open a demo seller property and click **Buy / Contact Seller**.
7. Enter buyer phone/email/message and submit the inquiry.
8. Return to the demo seller workspace.
9. Open **Buyer Inquiries** and show the new inquiry/notification.
10. Open the inquiry to show buyer details.
11. Reply from the seller side.
12. Return to the buyer session and open **Inbox** to show the seller response.

This demonstrates that EstateLens is not only a property search interface; it also connects a buyer to the seller through a database-backed inquiry and messaging workflow.

## Robust review routing

For review reliability, the preloaded academic properties (including the original property IDs 101–120 and 201–206) are owned by the dedicated **EstateLens Demo Seller** account. This means a reviewer can select a familiar preloaded property such as **Sea Breeze 2BHK (property 102, MVP Colony)** and the inquiry will reach the Demo Seller Inbox without needing to locate a separate seller account first.
